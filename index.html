Who We Are

Our Story

Moroganics brings authentic Moroccan olive oil to homes across the United States. Our oil is harvested in Fquih Ben Salah, Morocco, a region known for its rich soil and ideal olive-growing climate.

By working with trusted agricultural cooperatives, we ensure every bottle reflects tradition, quality, and authenticity. Each drop captures centuries of Mediterranean heritage, delivering unmatched flavor and purity to your table.

Our Journey

From Morocco to Your Table

01

Harvested in Morocco

Olives are carefully handpicked from the sun-drenched groves of Fquih Ben Salah at peak ripeness.

02

Extracted & Packed

Cold extracted and bottled by Cooperative Agricole Al Karama, Fquih Ben Salah, ensuring quality at the source.

03

Imported to the USA

Imported by Moroganics LLC, Worcester, Massachusetts, with careful handling throughout the journey.

04

Delivered to You

Fresh, authentic Moroccan olive oil arrives at your door — ready to elevate every meal you create.
