Frequently Asked Questions

Moroganics olive oil is sourced exclusively from the Fquih Ben Salah region in Morocco, known for its rich soil and ideal olive-growing climate. Our oil is cold extracted by Cooperative Agricole Al Karama, preserving the natural flavor, nutrients, and antioxidants. Every bottle is 100% pure extra virgin olive oil with no additives or blending.

Our olive oil is harvested from olive groves in Fquih Ben Salah, Morocco — a region in the Beni Mellal-Khénifra area with a long tradition of premium olive cultivation. The olives are extracted and packed locally by Cooperative Agricole Al Karama before being imported to the United States.

Cold extraction means the olive oil is processed at temperatures below 27°C (80°F). This method preserves the oil's natural flavors, aromas, and nutritional properties — including polyphenols and antioxidants — that can be diminished by heat-based extraction methods.

We currently offer three sizes: 250ml, 500ml, and 1 Liter bottles of Extra Virgin Olive Oil. Each size delivers the same premium quality and authentic Moroccan flavor.
